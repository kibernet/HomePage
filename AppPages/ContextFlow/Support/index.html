Frequently Asked Questions

How do I enable the Finder extension?

After installing ContextFlow, macOS may ask you to enable the Finder extension in System Settings. Open System Settings, find Login Items & Extensions or Extensions, and make sure ContextFlow is enabled for Finder.

Why does the right-click menu not appear in Finder?

Confirm that the Finder extension is enabled, then relaunch Finder or restart your Mac. Also check ContextFlow settings to make sure the menu scope includes the location where you are using Finder.

Does ContextFlow upload my files?

No. ContextFlow performs file actions locally on your Mac after you invoke a Finder right-click command. It does not upload selected files, copied paths, checksums, or encrypted DMG files.

How do batch rename actions work?

Batch rename workflows provide a preview before changes are applied. Review the preview carefully, then confirm the operation only when the result is correct.

How are encrypted DMG files created?

ContextFlow uses Apple's macOS system tool for user-initiated AES-256 encrypted DMG creation. You choose the selected files, password, and output location.

Can I change the Terminal app or interface language?

Yes. Open ContextFlow preferences to choose terminal behavior, menu options, Finder scope, completion alerts, and app language.
