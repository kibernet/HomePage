ContextFlow for Mac

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 27, 2026

ContextFlow is a local-first macOS Finder right-click productivity tool. It helps you create files, copy paths, batch rename items, create encrypted DMG files, copy checksums, use AirDrop, open Terminal, and open selected files with professional apps.

Data We Collect

ContextFlow does not collect personal data. The app does not require an account, does not include advertising SDKs, does not include analytics SDKs, and does not include third-party tracking SDKs.

Data Stored on Your Mac

ContextFlow stores app preferences locally on your Mac so the main app and Finder extension can share settings. This may include:

  • Enabled or disabled Finder menu actions.
  • Finder menu scope, such as Home, common folders, or all disks.
  • Terminal app preference, default rename prefix, completion alert preference, and selected app language.
  • Other local settings required to keep the app and Finder extension behavior consistent.

These settings remain on your device. ContextFlow does not send them to a server.

File Access

ContextFlow acts only on files or folders that you select in Finder, or on the active Finder context when you invoke a right-click command. File operations are performed locally on your Mac. File paths may be copied to the clipboard only when you choose a copy-path command.

Checksums and Encrypted DMG Files

ContextFlow can compute SHA-256 and MD5 checksums locally for selected files. It can also create user-initiated AES-256 encrypted DMG files through Apple's macOS system tools. File contents are not uploaded by these features.

Network Access

ContextFlow does not require network access for its core features. The app has no account system and no cloud service dependency.

Data Deletion

You can remove local preferences by deleting the app and its related local settings from your Mac. Files created, renamed, copied, moved, exported, or packaged by ContextFlow remain wherever you chose to save them and can be managed in Finder.

Children's Privacy

ContextFlow is a general productivity utility and is not directed to children. The app does not knowingly collect information from children or any other users.
