Frequently Asked Questions

Does ClearWatermark upload my photos?

No. ClearWatermark processes selected images, masks, and repair results locally on your device.

What images should I use with ClearWatermark?

Use ClearWatermark only with images you own, have licensed, or otherwise have permission to edit. Do not use it to remove copyrighted watermarks, platform marks, or rights notices you do not have permission to modify.

How do I repair an image?

Import a photo, brush over the area you want to repair, tap or click Repair, compare the result, and export or save the edited image.

Why does processing take time?

Image repair runs on device using the bundled Core ML model. Processing time can vary depending on image size, mask size, and device performance.

Why does Photos permission appear on iPhone or iPad?

Photos permission is used only when you choose an image from your library or save an edited result to Photos.

Why does file access appear on Mac?

File access is used only for files you explicitly choose through the system file picker, drag and drop, or export panel.
