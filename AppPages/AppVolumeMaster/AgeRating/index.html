Content Summary

Category AppVolumeMaster Status

Intended Use

AppVolumeMaster helps users view audio-active apps, mute one app, mute all, restore all, use solo mode, remember per-app audio preferences, launch at login, and copy diagnostics for troubleshooting. These features are utility-oriented and do not expose users to external content.

Privacy and Safety

AppVolumeMaster does not require an account, does not include tracking SDKs, and stores app preferences locally on the user's Mac. Audio is processed locally and is not uploaded. Read the privacy policy here: Privacy Policy.
