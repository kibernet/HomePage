AppVolumeMaster for Mac

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 27, 2026

AppVolumeMaster, localized as 声控大师 in Simplified Chinese, is a local-first Mac menu bar utility for controlling audio behavior by application. It is designed to work without accounts, cloud sync, advertising, analytics, or tracking.

Data We Collect

AppVolumeMaster does not collect personal data. The app does not require sign-in, does not operate a remote service, does not include advertising SDKs, and does not include third-party tracking or analytics SDKs.

Data Stored on Your Mac

To provide the app's core features, AppVolumeMaster stores preferences locally on your Mac. This may include:

  • Per-application mute and volume preference choices.
  • Search, filter, panel, and app display preferences.
  • Launch at login and other local settings selected by you.
  • Diagnostic text that you explicitly copy when requesting support.

This data remains on your device. AppVolumeMaster does not upload these preferences to a server.

Audio Handling

AppVolumeMaster uses macOS audio APIs to identify audio-producing processes and apply local audio control behavior. Audio is processed locally on your Mac. The app does not record audio files, upload audio content, sell audio data, or share audio content with third parties.

System Permissions

AppVolumeMaster may request system audio capture permission so it can identify and control audio-producing apps. This permission is used only for local audio control features.

Network Access

AppVolumeMaster has no account system and no cloud service dependency. The current app does not use networking to collect user data.

Data Deletion

You can remove AppVolumeMaster's local preferences by deleting the app's local settings from your Mac. Any diagnostic information is shared only when you choose to copy and send it yourself.

Children's Privacy

AppVolumeMaster is a general utility and is not directed to children. The app does not knowingly collect information from children or any other users.

Changes to This Policy
