CineCapture Pro for macOS

Age Rating Information

Last updated: May 27, 2026

CineCapture Pro is a professional screen recording, camera recording, editing, and creator workflow utility for macOS. It does not contain entertainment content, social networking features, gambling, advertising, user-generated public feeds, or in-app purchases.

Recommended App Store Age Rating

CineCapture Pro is intended for general audiences and professional users. Based on the app's functionality, the expected rating is suitable for ages 4+ where available, subject to Apple's final App Store Connect questionnaire and review.

Content Notes

  • No explicit, mature, horror, gambling, or simulated gambling content is included in the app.
  • No unrestricted web browsing is included.
  • No social networking or public user profile system is included.
  • The app records content selected by the user. Users are responsible for ensuring they have permission to record their own screen, audio, camera, and any content displayed on their Mac.
