CineCapturePro for macOS

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 21, 2026

CineCapturePro (also referenced in some macOS and App Store metadata as CineCapture Pro) is a local-first macOS recording studio for screen recording, window and app capture, system audio, microphone audio, camera picture-in-picture, recording library management, subtitles, chapters, exports, and workspace archives.

Summary: CineCapturePro does not require an account, does not include advertising SDKs, does not include third-party tracking SDKs, and does not upload recordings to developer-operated servers. Recordings, exports, thumbnails, subtitles, and library data are stored locally on your Mac by default.

Data We Collect

The current version of CineCapturePro does not collect, upload, sell, or share personal data through developer-operated online services. The app does not provide a developer account system, cloud synchronization, advertising, third-party analytics, crash-reporting SDKs, or third-party tracking SDKs.

Data Stored Locally on Your Mac

To provide recording and post-production workflows, CineCapturePro may store or process the following data locally on your Mac:

  • App settings, presets, selected language, theme, output folder, window state, workspace bookmarks, and recording preferences.
  • Recording files, video tracks, system audio, microphone audio, camera picture-in-picture media, thumbnails, waveform data, subtitles, chapters, review notes, and exported files.
  • Recording library records such as file path, file size, duration, codec, container, resolution, frame rate, HDR state, tags, notes, and sidecar JSON metadata.
  • Diagnostic reports, recovery markers, workspace archives, workflow profile JSON manifests, and local plugin configuration that you add or create.

CineCapturePro asks for Apple system permissions only when you use features that require them:

Recordings, Exports, and Library Files

Recordings, audio tracks, thumbnails, subtitles, exported files, and archive files are saved to locations you choose or to local folders provided by macOS, such as the Movies folder. CineCapturePro does not automatically upload these files and does not proxy them through developer-operated servers.

Diagnostics and Privacy Manifest

The app reads necessary system information locally for recording readiness checks and performance display, such as available disk space, file timestamps, app preferences, recording timers, system boot time, and preview throttling. These uses align with the app's PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy privacy manifest and Required Reason API declarations, including UserDefaults, FileTimestamp, DiskSpace, and SystemBootTime. They are used only for local app functionality, not tracking.

Support Requests

If you contact us by email for support, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address, issue description, screenshots, logs, or diagnostic details. Please do not send passwords, private keys, sensitive recordings, confidential screenshots, or files that you do not want us to review. Support information is used only to respond to your request.

Third-Party Services

The current version does not include analytics, advertising networks, third-party tracking SDKs, cloud synchronization, payment SDKs, social sign-in, or developer-operated AI upload services. If a future version adds optional online AI, transcription, cloud storage, subscriptions, or server-side processing, this policy and App Store privacy declarations will be updated before release.

User-Initiated Sharing

You control any sharing of recordings, exports, screenshots, reports, or workspace archives through Finder, email, cloud drives, messaging apps, video platforms, or other services outside CineCapturePro. Those services are governed by their own privacy policies.

Data Deletion and Retention

You can remove library records inside CineCapturePro, and you can delete recordings, sidecar JSON, thumbnails, subtitles, exported files, workspace archives, and local workflow profiles in Finder. Uninstalling the app removes the app itself, but recordings and exports saved in output folders or other locations remain until you delete them.

Children's Privacy

CineCapturePro is a productivity tool for creators, education, product demos, and professional recording workflows. It is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect information from children or any other users.
