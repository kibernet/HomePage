CineCapture Pro for macOS

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 27, 2026

CineCapture Pro is a professional macOS recording studio for screen, window, app, audio, camera, editing, export, and creator workflow tasks. The app is designed as a local-first Mac application.

Data We Collect

CineCapture Pro does not collect personal data through a hosted service. The app does not require account sign-in, does not include advertising SDKs, and does not include third-party tracking SDKs.

Data Stored Locally on Your Mac

To provide recording and creator workflow features, CineCapture Pro may store local app data on your Mac, including:

  • Recording settings, presets, theme settings, output folder preferences, and capture configuration.
  • Recent recording library metadata, thumbnails, tags, sidecar JSON files, subtitles, insight drafts, export records, and diagnostic reports.
  • Local plugin manifests and local workspace archives that you create or install.

These files remain on your Mac unless you choose to move, export, share, or upload them using another tool.

Screen, Camera, Microphone, and Accessibility Permissions

CineCapture Pro uses Apple system permissions only for app features you choose to use:

  • Screen Recording permission is used to capture displays, windows, and apps.
  • Microphone permission is used for voice recording and audio meters.
  • Camera permission is used for camera preview and picture-in-picture workflows.
  • Accessibility permission may be used for advanced shortcuts and keyboard overlay features.

These permissions do not send your recordings or device content to us.

Recordings and Exports

Your videos, audio tracks, thumbnails, subtitles, exports, and archive files are saved to folders on your Mac that you select or that macOS provides, such as the Movies folder. CineCapture Pro does not automatically upload recordings.

AI and Draft Workflow Features

The current app can create local draft transcript, chapter, highlight, and insight files. If a future version adds optional online AI services, that feature will be clearly disclosed before use and this policy will be updated.

Data Deletion

You can delete recordings, sidecar files, exports, subtitle files, insight files, plugin manifests, and workspace archives from Finder. You can also remove recent library records from inside the app.

Children's Privacy

CineCapture Pro is a professional productivity and creator utility. It is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised update date.
