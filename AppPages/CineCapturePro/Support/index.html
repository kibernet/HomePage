Frequently Asked Questions

Where are recordings saved?

Recordings are saved to the output folder selected in the app. By default, macOS may use your Movies folder. You can change the output folder from Settings or the recording control panel.

Why does recording require permissions?

macOS requires explicit permission for screen recording, microphone access, camera access, and some advanced shortcut or overlay features. Open the app's Permissions or Diagnostics section to review permission status.

Does CineCapture Pro upload my videos?

No. Recordings, thumbnails, subtitles, metadata, exports, and archives are stored locally unless you choose to share or upload them with another service.

Why is a window or app missing from the source list?

Refresh sources in the recording panel. Some windows may not be capturable due to macOS privacy rules, DRM, system restrictions, or app-specific behavior.

How do I record camera picture-in-picture?

Select a screen plus camera mode, choose a camera device, and enable Camera PIP in Compositing settings. The app can composite the camera PIP into the final video output.

How do I check recording quality?

The library includes quality control status for completed recordings, including media duration, track presence, and file size checks. Use Diagnostics for broader hardware and storage checks.

How do I export subtitles or insights?

Open the Editor section, select a recording, generate draft transcript or insights, then export SRT or Markdown files. Draft AI workflow files are local project artifacts.
