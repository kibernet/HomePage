PanePilot for Mac

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 27, 2026

PanePilot is a local-first window layout assistant for macOS. It helps you arrange selected windows, restore workspace snapshots, use custom split ratios, and apply professional layout presets without accounts, cloud sync, advertising, or tracking.

Data We Collect

PanePilot does not collect personal data. The app does not require sign-in, does not operate a remote service, does not include advertising SDKs, and does not include third-party tracking or analytics SDKs.

Data Processed on Your Mac

To perform user-triggered window layout actions, PanePilot may process the following information locally on your Mac:

  • Application names and bundle identifiers.
  • Window titles.
  • Window positions, sizes, display information, and visible screen areas.
  • User-created layout templates, workspace snapshots, selected presets, and app preferences.

This information is used only to arrange windows, match windows to layout slots, restore saved workspaces, and provide undo behavior.

Accessibility Permission

PanePilot uses macOS Accessibility permission to read window metadata and adjust window positions. Without this permission, the app can open its control panel but cannot perform window arrangement actions. PanePilot does not read window contents and does not record keyboard input.

Network Access

PanePilot is designed to work locally on your Mac. It does not upload window titles, app lists, templates, snapshots, or other app data to a server.

Data Deletion

You can remove saved layouts, workspace snapshots, and preferences from within the app where available, or by deleting the app's local preference data from macOS. Uninstalling the app does not create a remote deletion request because PanePilot does not maintain server-side user accounts or cloud data.

Children's Privacy

PanePilot is a general productivity utility and is not directed to children. The app does not knowingly collect information from children or any other users.

Changes to This Policy
