TimeFocus for Mac

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 27, 2026

TimeFocus is a local-first focus timer for Mac. It is designed to help you manage focus sessions, breaks, projects, templates, and lightweight statistics without accounts, cloud sync, advertising, or tracking.

Data We Collect

TimeFocus does not collect personal data. The app does not require sign-in, does not operate a remote service, does not include advertising SDKs, and does not include third-party tracking SDKs.

Data Stored on Your Mac

To provide the app's core features, TimeFocus stores app data locally on your Mac. This may include:

  • Timer settings, such as focus duration, break duration, long break interval, and daily goal.
  • Focus session records, such as start time, end time, session type, project label, and completion state.
  • Project labels, selected templates, app language, hotkey preferences, and panel display settings.
  • Window position, panel mode, and current timer runtime state when restore is enabled.

This data remains on your device unless you choose to export it. TimeFocus can export CSV files and local backup files when you explicitly select those actions.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, TimeFocus uses macOS local notifications to remind you when a focus or break session ends. Notification permission is used only for local reminders.

Network Access

TimeFocus has no account system and no cloud service dependency. The app is designed to work locally on your Mac.

Data Deletion

You can clear focus statistics from the app's Settings and Statistics window. You can also delete exported CSV or backup files from any location where you saved them.

Children's Privacy

TimeFocus is a general productivity utility and is not directed to children. The app does not knowingly collect information from children or any other users.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised update date.
