Frequently Asked Questions

Does TimeFocus upload my focus records?

No. TimeFocus is local-first. Focus records, projects, settings, and statistics are stored on your Mac.

Why are global shortcuts not working?

If another app already uses the same shortcut, TimeFocus may not be able to register it. Close the conflicting app or control the timer from the floating panel or menu bar.

How do I back up my data?

Open Settings and Statistics, go to the Statistics section, and use the backup option. TimeFocus exports a local JSON backup file that you can restore later.

How do I export focus records?

Open Settings and Statistics, go to the Statistics section, and use CSV export. The CSV contains focus session records and does not include device identifiers or account information.

How do I turn off reminders?

Open the Timer settings section and disable system notifications or completion sounds.

How do I reset statistics?

Open Settings and Statistics, go to the Statistics section, and use the reset statistics action. This clears local focus statistics in the app.
