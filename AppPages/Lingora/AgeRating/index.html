Lingora for iPhone, iPad, and Mac

Age Rating Information

Last updated: June 5, 2026

Lingora is an education app for language learning. It provides language catalogs, vocabulary decks, review practice, pronunciation support, local learning statistics, and optional App Store purchases.

Recommended App Store Age Rating: 4+

Intended Use

Lingora is intended for general audiences who want to learn or review languages. The app's content is educational and does not expose users to public user-generated content, social feeds, or unrestricted browsing.

Privacy and Safety

Lingora does not require a developer account, does not include advertising SDKs, and does not include third-party tracking SDKs. Read the privacy policy here: Privacy Policy.
