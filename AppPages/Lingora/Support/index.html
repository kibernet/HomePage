Frequently Asked Questions

What is Lingora?

Lingora is a language-learning app with a global language catalog, structured vocabulary paths, review practice, pronunciation support, and progress insights.

Which platforms are supported?

Lingora is prepared for iPhone, iPad, and Mac App Store product pages. Availability may depend on the version distributed through the App Store.

Does Lingora require an account?

No. Lingora does not require a developer account or external server login for the core learning experience.

How do I restore purchases?

If you purchased Lingora Pro through the App Store, use the restore purchase action inside the app. Purchases are processed by Apple StoreKit and are tied to your Apple ID.

Why is microphone permission requested?

Microphone permission may be requested for pronunciation and speaking practice. You can deny or disable this permission from system settings if you do not want to use speech practice.

How do I change my learning language?

Open the app's language catalog or profile-related settings, then choose the language path you want to study.

How do I report incorrect language content?

Email kibernet@sina.com with the language name, the word or phrase, and what you believe should be corrected.
