Lingora for iPhone, iPad, and Mac

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 5, 2026

Lingora is a global language-learning app. It helps users browse language catalogs, create learning paths, practice vocabulary and pronunciation, review cards, and track learning progress.

Lingora is designed to be privacy-conscious. The app does not require a developer account, does not include advertising SDKs, and does not include third-party tracking SDKs.

Data We Collect

The developer does not collect personal data from Lingora users through a developer-operated server. Lingora does not require sign-in with a Lingora account and does not sell user data.

Data Stored on Your Device

To provide the app's core features, Lingora stores learning data locally on your device. This may include:

  • Selected native language, learning languages, learning objective, and app language preference.
  • Vocabulary decks, review progress, learning records, daily study statistics, and streak-related data.
  • Practice settings, display settings, and other app preferences.

This data is used only to operate the app's learning experience on your device.

iCloud and Apple Services

If a future version or enabled system setting uses Apple iCloud or CloudKit features, learning data may be stored or synced through the user's Apple account infrastructure. Apple services are governed by Apple's own terms and privacy policies.

Purchases

Lingora may offer Lingora Pro subscriptions or lifetime access. Purchases are processed by Apple's StoreKit system. The developer receives purchase entitlement status needed to unlock app features, but payment details are handled by Apple.

Microphone and Speech Recognition

Lingora may request microphone or speech recognition permission for pronunciation and speaking practice. These permissions are used only when you use speech-related learning features. Lingora does not use microphone access for advertising or tracking.

Notifications

If Lingora adds or uses local reminders, notification permission is used only for app-related learning reminders or practice prompts.

Third-Party Advertising and Tracking

Lingora does not include third-party advertising SDKs and does not track users across other companies' apps or websites.

Children's Privacy

Lingora is a general education app and is not directed to collect personal information from children. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Deletion

You can remove app data by deleting the app from your device. If Apple iCloud sync is enabled for the app in the future, you may also need to manage related iCloud data from your Apple account or device settings.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised update date.
