Frequently Asked Questions

Does ClipForge Pro upload my videos?

No. Media probing, compression, conversion, audio extraction, trimming, rotating, cropping, and queue processing happen locally on your Mac.

Why does the first export ask for folder permission?

macOS sandboxing requires the app to use user-selected file and folder permissions. Choose an output folder that you can write to, such as your Movies folder.

What should I do if an export fails?

Try the HD MP4 preset first. If the issue continues, use Export Diagnostics in the app and email the generated report. The report remains local unless you choose to send it.

Does the app require Homebrew or command-line FFmpeg?

No. The Mac App Store package is designed to bundle FFmpeg and ffprobe, so users do not need Homebrew or command-line setup.

Which formats are supported?

The app supports common workflows including MP4, MOV, M4V, MKV, WebM, AVI, MPEG-TS, FLV, M4A, MP3, AAC, WAV, FLAC, AIFF, CAF, Opus, OGG/Vorbis, and ALAC, subject to the selected source file and encoder support.

常见问题

影锻会上传我的视频吗？

不会。媒体分析、压缩、转换、音频提取、剪辑、旋转、裁切和队列处理都在你的 Mac 本机完成。

为什么需要选择输出文件夹？

macOS 沙盒要求 App 使用用户主动选择的文件和文件夹权限。请选择你有写入权限的输出目录。

导出失败怎么办？

可以先尝试 HD MP4 方案。如果仍然失败，在 App 中导出诊断报告并发送到 kibernet@sina.com。诊断报告不会自动上传。
