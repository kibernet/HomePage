ClipForge Pro for Mac / 影锻

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 27, 2026

ClipForge Pro is a local video compression, conversion, audio extraction, and lightweight editing tool for Mac. The app is designed to process user-selected media files on your device without accounts, cloud upload, advertising, or tracking.

Data We Collect

ClipForge Pro does not collect personal data. The app does not require sign-in, does not operate a user account service, does not include advertising SDKs, and does not include third-party tracking SDKs.

Media Files

The app can access only the video, audio, folder, and output locations that you select through macOS file pickers, drag and drop, or saved security-scoped permissions. Media processing is performed locally on your Mac. Your source media and exported files are not uploaded by the app.

Data Stored on Your Mac

To provide app features, ClipForge Pro may store local preferences on your Mac, including:

  • Default output folder and security-scoped folder permission.
  • App language, export settings, target size mode, and queue preferences.
  • Local diagnostic logs and reports only when generated or exported by the user.

These records remain on your device unless you choose to share an exported file or diagnostic report.

Network Access

Core app functionality works offline. Open-source license links or support links may open external websites only when you choose to click them.

Open Source Components

ClipForge Pro bundles FFmpeg and ffprobe for local media probing and transcoding. The app includes open-source notices, license information, build notes, and source package materials in the app bundle for compliance purposes.

Children's Privacy

ClipForge Pro is a general media utility and is not directed to children. The app does not knowingly collect information from children or any other users.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised update date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at kibernet@sina.com.

影锻是一款 Mac 本地视频压缩、格式转换、音频提取和轻量剪辑工具。App 处理用户主动选择的本地媒体文件，不需要账号，不上传云端，不包含广告 SDK，也不包含第三方追踪 SDK。

我们收集的数据

影锻不收集个人数据。App 不提供账号系统，不运营远程用户服务，不集成广告或追踪 SDK。

媒体文件
