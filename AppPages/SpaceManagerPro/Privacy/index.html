SpaceManagerPro for Mac

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 5, 2026

SpaceManagerPro is a local-first macOS menu bar utility for organizing desktop workspace profiles, switching between Spaces, recording hotkeys, and saving optional window layout snapshots. It is designed to work without accounts, cloud sync, advertising, analytics, or tracking.

Data We Collect

SpaceManagerPro does not collect personal data. The app does not require sign-in, does not operate a developer-hosted account service, does not include advertising SDKs, and does not include third-party tracking or analytics SDKs.

Data Stored on Your Mac

To provide its core features, SpaceManagerPro stores app data locally on your Mac. This may include:

  • Workspace profile names, icons, colors, notes, display mappings, pinned status, and sort order.
  • Global hotkey preferences, recent workspace choices, and app settings.
  • Window layout snapshots, including app names, bundle identifiers, window titles, frame positions, sizes, and related display information.
  • Configuration backup files that you explicitly export from the app.

This data remains on your device unless you choose to export, back up, or share it yourself.

macOS Permissions

SpaceManagerPro may ask for macOS Accessibility permission. This permission is used only for user-requested workspace switching commands and optional window layout features, such as reading window positions when saving a layout snapshot and restoring saved window positions when you choose to restore a layout.

Network Access

The current version does not require network access for its core features. SpaceManagerPro has no account system and no cloud service dependency.

Payments

If SpaceManagerPro is purchased through the Mac App Store, payment processing is handled by Apple. The developer does not receive your full payment card details.

Data Deletion

You can remove local workspace profiles, settings, layout snapshots, and exported backup files from your Mac. Deleting the app and its local app data removes the data stored by SpaceManagerPro on that device.

Children's Privacy

SpaceManagerPro is a general productivity utility and is not directed to children. The app does not knowingly collect information from children or any other users.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised update date.
