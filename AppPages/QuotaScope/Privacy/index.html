QuotaScope for Mac

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 25, 2026

QuotaScope does not collect personal data and does not operate a developer-controlled cloud service.

Data We Collect

The developer does not collect data from this app. There are no advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or tracking SDKs.

Local Data

Account labels, account identifiers, cached usage snapshots, refresh settings, and display preferences are stored locally on your Mac or in the app group container.

Tokens and Credentials

Tokens are stored in macOS Keychain when available and are used only to refresh usage data for accounts you add.

File Access

QuotaScope only reads local JSON files that you explicitly select through the macOS file picker.

Deletion

You can remove accounts inside the app and delete local app data from your Mac. The developer does not maintain a server-side QuotaScope account.
