CodexGauge for Mac

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 19, 2026

CodexGauge is a macOS utility for monitoring Codex/OpenAI account usage, reset countdowns, and quota pressure across multiple accounts.

Data We Collect

CodexGauge does not collect personal data, does not include advertising SDKs, and does not include third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

Local Data

Account aliases, account identifiers, cached usage snapshots, selected language, and display preferences are stored locally on your Mac or in the configured App Group container.

Tokens and Credentials

Access tokens and refresh tokens are stored in macOS Keychain when available. The app uses them only to request usage information for accounts you add.

Network Access

CodexGauge connects to OpenAI/ChatGPT usage endpoints to refresh quota data. It does not operate a developer-controlled cloud service.

Deletion

You can remove accounts inside the app and delete local app data from your Mac. No server-side CodexGauge account is maintained by the developer.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact kibernet@sina.com.
