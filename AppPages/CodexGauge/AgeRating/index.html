CodexUsageDashboard for Mac

Age Rating Information

Last updated: July 1, 2026

CodexUsageDashboard is a developer productivity utility for monitoring Codex account quota windows, reset countdowns, and subscription expiry dates. Recommended App Store age rating: 4+.

Content

  • No mature, violent, sexual, gambling, or medical content.
  • No user-generated public content.
  • No ads, in-app purchases, or external commerce.
  • No unrestricted web browsing or embedded social features.

Accounts and Network Access

CodexUsageDashboard can open a browser-based authorization flow when the user chooses to add or reauthorize an account. It connects to the account provider's authorization, usage, and billing-related endpoints only to authenticate, retrieve quota data, and read subscription metadata for accounts the user adds.

Privacy

CodexUsageDashboard stores credentials locally in macOS Keychain when available and stores cached quota snapshots locally for the app and widget. Read the Privacy Policy for details.
