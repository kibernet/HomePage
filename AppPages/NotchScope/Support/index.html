Frequently Asked Questions

What does NotchScope show?

NotchScope shows glanceable system information around the MacBook notch, including network speed, CPU usage, memory pressure, battery state, weather, and local warning alerts.

Does NotchScope require an account?

No. NotchScope does not require sign-in or an account. System metrics and settings are processed locally on your Mac.

Why is weather unavailable?

Weather is optional and uses Open-Meteo. Check your network connection, confirm that weather is enabled, and verify that the configured city or coordinates are valid.

Why are network speeds different from another app?

Network meters may use different interfaces, sampling windows, or unit formatting. NotchScope includes a network source selection setting so you can choose automatic physical-interface mode or another available source.

Why does the notch panel hide in full-screen apps?

NotchScope includes full-screen awareness so the panel can stay out of the way while you use full-screen apps. You can adjust panel visibility and behavior in the app settings.

How do I send diagnostics?

Use the feedback or diagnostics action in the app. NotchScope opens an editable email draft, and diagnostics are only sent if you choose to send that email.
