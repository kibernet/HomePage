NotchScope for Mac

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 10, 2026

NotchScope is a local-first macOS utility that turns the MacBook notch area into a glanceable system monitor for network speed, CPU, memory pressure, battery, weather, and local alerts.

Data We Collect

NotchScope does not require an account, does not include advertising SDKs, and does not track users across apps or websites. The app does not sell personal data.

Data Processed on Your Mac

To provide its core features, NotchScope processes system status and preferences locally on your Mac. This may include:

  • CPU usage, memory status, network throughput, battery level, and charging state.
  • Panel visibility, display style, metric selection, warning thresholds, and other app settings.
  • Cached weather display state and configured weather location when weather is enabled.
  • User-controlled diagnostics details if you choose to prepare a support email.

These values are used to display the notch panel, preferences, and local warning states. They are not uploaded to a NotchScope account or cloud service.

Weather

If weather is enabled, NotchScope rounds the configured latitude and longitude coordinates to two decimal places before storing them and before sending them to Open-Meteo to fetch current weather data. Open-Meteo may receive the request IP address as part of normal internet operation. Weather is optional and can be disabled in the app.

You can configure weather by searching for a city, entering coordinates manually, or choosing Use Current Location. Current location is requested only after you choose that option and grant macOS location permission. NotchScope stores only the rounded coordinates locally for weather display and does not use continuous background location tracking.

Diagnostics and Feedback

NotchScope can create a diagnostics report for support. Diagnostics are not sent automatically. If you choose to send feedback, your email app opens a draft that you can review, edit, or delete before sending.

Notifications and Local Alerts

NotchScope may show local warning states for CPU, memory, or battery thresholds based on your settings. These alerts are processed locally on your Mac.

Data Deletion

You can change or reset NotchScope settings from the app. You can also remove the app and its local preferences through standard macOS app and preference management.

Children's Privacy

NotchScope is a general productivity and utility app. It is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect information from children.
